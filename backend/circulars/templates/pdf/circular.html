{% extends "pdf/base_pdf.html" %} {% block content %} {% comment %} The circular PDF (Phase 50). Section order is the brief's: header, reference, subject, department, issue date, content, broadcast summary, acknowledgement summary, archive information. Two things distinguish it from the memo PDF and both are deliberate. First, a circular's "signatures" are one line, not a grid of certification blocks. A memo collects a chain of approvals and each one is a distinct act worth its own block; a circular is ISSUED by one person on one date, and printing a five-card approval grid for it would overstate what happened. Second, the registers are conditional on `show_registers`. A recipient exporting their own copy is entitled to the circular and to the summary figures; they are not entitled to a list naming which colleagues have not read it. The view decides that and this template obeys it - the boundary lives in permissions.can_view_registers, in one place, not in two. A comment TAG rather than a hash comment: Django's {# #} is single-line only, and a multi-line one prints onto the face of the document. That has already happened once in this codebase. {% endcomment %}
